# 1. VISION

OVOLL is a premium digital product studio focused on branding, design,
websites, software engineering, AI and growth.

Visitors should immediately perceive:

-   Precision
-   Trust
-   Intelligence
-   Modernity
-   Premium craftsmanship
-   Technical excellence

The website should feel closer to products like Apple, Linear, Stripe,
Framer and Vercel than to a conventional marketing agency.

# 4. LOGO SYSTEM

The OVOLL symbol is the foundation of the visual language.

Reuse its geometry for:

-   Hero particles
-   Background graphics
-   Orbital rings
-   Section dividers
-   Decorative lines
-   Preloader
-   Cursor effects
-   Icons
-   Motion paths

Avoid unrelated geometric objects.

# 29. MASTER AI DIRECTIVE

Do NOT redesign the information architecture.

Do NOT remove existing functionality.

Instead:

Integrate the complete OVOLL brand system.

Use the official palette consistently.

Use the logo as the basis of particles, motion, decorative graphics and
3D.

Upgrade every page until it feels handcrafted.

Eliminate generic UI.

Every animation must have purpose.

Every interaction must reinforce trust.

The final result should feel like a premium global product company
rather than a conventional agency website.
