# OVOLL --- Brand Identity

## Brand Position

OVOLL builds brands, digital products and growth systems.

## Personality

-   Intelligent
-   Premium
-   Precise
-   Modern
-   Human

## Logo Usage

The logo is the foundation of: - Hero animation - Particle systems -
Background patterns - Dividers - Loader - Motion paths

## Color Usage

60% Deep Navy 20% White 10% Teal 5% Electric Cyan 5% Neutral

Teal = interaction Cyan = motion

## Visual Language

-   Glass morphism
-   Orbital graphics
-   Particle trails
-   Gradient meshes
-   Soft glow
-   Editorial whitespace

## Voice

Clear, confident, concise. Avoid buzzwords and exaggerated marketing
language.

## Recognition Test

If the logo is hidden, users should still recognize OVOLL through color,
motion, spacing, typography and interaction.
